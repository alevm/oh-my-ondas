Prototype v1: Field Unit

Concept · A5 footprint (≈ 210 × 120 mm) · Indian temple stucco palette · every control is a part we own or have on order

Concept for review, not a final enclosure. Sizes follow the real parts (2.8″ LCD module, 45 mm faders, EC11 knobs, 0.96″ OLED). The encoder spacing still has to match the Quad encoder boards. Tap or hover over a control to see which part and bus it uses.

Field Unit v1

Tap or hover over any control. Left: hands-on navigation and the faders. Centre: screen and radio window. Right: the sound pads and radio tuning.

Design ideas

  • Radio you can see: a telescopic antenna and a dedicated radio window (the two-colour OLED, yellow status band over a blue frequency) with a larger TUNE knob, so the radio feels like part of the instrument rather than a menu.
  • Game-pad navigation: 5-way switch plus REC and PLAY under the left thumb, MODE and SHIFT beside it, the way small handhelds such as Casio's SX-C1 sampler put game-controller buttons on top.
  • Direct controls, few menus: each source has its own colour, its own fader and its own knob ring (the Quad encoder LEDs). The same colours run through the LCD lanes, in the spirit of "no menus, just buttons" pocket samplers such as the Stylophone VOICE.
  • Pocket density, hand-sized parts: everything fits on an A5 plate while keeping real 45 mm faders and touch pads big enough for fingers (24 × 18 mm copper).
  • Place on screen: the LCD footer shows where and when (GPS) with the tempo, because every capture is stamped with location.

Palette: Indian temple stucco

Taken from the painted stucco figures of South Indian temple towers (gopurams): a peacock-turquoise body with a vermilion and gold outline, and one temple colour per sound source.

Open questions

  • Encoder spacing is set by the Quad boards: measure them and adjust the two knob rows.
  • Front panel material over the pads: touch works through 1–3 mm of plastic.
  • Power bank outside the case, on the back: printed cradle or strap, cable slot for 2 short USB cables.
