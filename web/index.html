Oh My Ondas

A portable instrument that listens to a place — microphone, contact piezo, AM/FM/shortwave radio and GPS — and turns what it hears into music. Every capture is stamped with where and when it was made.

Try the web app See the hardware Gallery

Field Unit v1 concept · A5 · Indian temple stucco palette

Three ways to play

Picture

A snapshot of the sound of a place at one moment.

Soundscape

The engine listens to the place and composes from it, by rules (generative, not machine learning).

Interact

Play live: 8-track sequencer, sampler with 8 pads, synth and effects.

Where it is now

HardwarePrototype #1 on two breadboards: Teensy 4.1 + Audio Shield. Working so far: audio playback, OLED and LCD; touch pads provisional, mic recording not tested yet. Next: faders, then encoders, GPS, mic and radio. Layout · Build log
