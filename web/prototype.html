Prototype v1 (Perma-Proto)

Full prototype #1 on one Perma-Proto + a front panel board (bench copy on the EIC-104 breadboard) · October 2026 · draft for review

Why this layout: with the Audio Shield under the Teensy, pins 0–23 sit inside the shield sockets. Everything here uses the pins past the shield edge (24–41, 3.3V, G). All UI parts share one I2C bus (Wire2), the LCD uses SPI1, and the Audio Shield keeps Wire (18/19) to itself. The LCD board stays off the breadboard (lesson from the white-screen debugging).
Replaces the earlier layout (v0.3: pins 0–23, 5″ ILI9488 on SPI0, 13 encoders, 19 buttons, joystick, NeoPixel ring), which assumed every Teensy pin was reachable. Purchases now live on the BOM page; the old diagram is in the git history.
Firmware: config.h (firmware v0.4.0) follows this layout: all UI I2C on Wire2, LCD on SPI1, 7 encoders on the two Quad boards, fader 5 on pin 40, ESP32-S2 on Serial7 (link protocol v4). Nav: the ANO I2C board (0x4B) or 5 buttons on MCP GPA0–4, read together; 4 buttons on GPB0–3. MPU9250 / AK8963 / BMP280 drivers on Wire2 are ready for when those parts are fitted. It builds; it has not run on the hardware yet. The assembly guide still describes the old v0.3 plan. Si4732 pins below are from the Skyworks datasheet (SOIC-16, Sept 2021); crystal pins from the PU2CLR Si4732-A10 reference schematic.

Pull-ups: each module brings its own (Adafruit boards 10 kΩ; the generic OLED, MPU9250 and BMP280 modules 4.7–10 kΩ). With everything fitted the bus sees about 1 kΩ: slightly strong, usually fine. Add none. If I2C errors appear, remove the OLED module’s pull-ups first.

Perma-Proto: the whole circuit on one board

ORDERED DigiKey One full-size Perma-Proto holds every chip and breakout. Check its row count on arrival (60 or 63): the plan below uses rows 1–60 either way. Only the front controls (5 nav buttons, 4 buttons, later the ANO) go on a small perfboard behind the panel. The OLED goes on the panel by 4 wires (its rows hold a connector); the MAX9814 can also move to the panel on 5 wires if the case needs it.

Bench copy first: build on the EIC-104 breadboard (2 full-size strips, numbered to row 63, same numbering as the Perma-Proto): same rows as below, rows 61–63 unused. One of the breadboards has a bad power rail: mark it and do not use its rails. When everything works, copy row for row onto the Perma-Proto.

Build order

  1. Before anything: PSRAM (ordered) soldered under the Teensy; Teensy onto the shield with the female 2×14 socket (headers: to buy, Diotronic).
  2. Rails and entry (row 1) on the EIC-104 bench copy. Measure 3.3 V on the rail.
  3. CAP1188 + OLED + LCD: the stack already verified, now in its final place. Run layer3_integration.
  4. ADS1115 + faders (Phase 4, layer4_faders_visual). Measure ADS1115 VDD = 3.3 V first.
  5. MCP23017 + 5 nav buttons + 4 buttons (layer5_controls_visual), then the Quad encoders with the EC11s (Phase 5).
  6. GPS on the STEMMA chain (Phase 6, layer5_encoders_gps_visual).
  7. ESP32-S2 link on Serial7 (Phase 7).
  8. Layer 1: MAX9814 mic → LINE-IN L (layer6_mic_visual), then the piezo buffer + MIC/PIEZO toggle, then the Si4732 radio → LINE-IN R once the 1207 adapter is here (Nov).
  9. Everything together: no I2C errors, clean audio. Then copy the layout 1:1 onto the Perma-Proto, and the buttons onto the front panel board.
  10. Later: ANO (Miami); IMU MPU9250; coil preamp + solar cell with the rotary switch; BMP280.
